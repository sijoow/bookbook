Style: a risograph-printed indie bookshop zine. Off-white paper with visible grain and halftone dots, printed with only three spot inks — fluorescent pink, cobalt blue and sunflower yellow — overlapping with slight misregistration. Bold chunky Korean display lettering, playful geometric planets and stacked-book shapes, stamp-like labels and stickers, energetic but readable. Flat print texture only. No gradients, no 3D, no emoji, no purple.
