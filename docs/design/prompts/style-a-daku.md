Style: a cozy Korean "dakku" scrapbook reading journal. Cream grid-paper background with subtle paper texture, strips of washi tape holding cards in place, die-cut sticker elements with thick white borders, hand-drawn ink-pen doodles of little stars and planets, pastel palette of apricot, mint, butter yellow, soft coral and warm brown ink. Rounded handwritten-style Korean lettering for headings, clean readable Korean sans for small text. The book tower, planets and portal paths look like cute paper stickers and pen drawings. Flat, tactile and charming. No smooth gradients, no glossy 3D renders, no emoji, no purple.
